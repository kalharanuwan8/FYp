# Participant information and consent — webcam distraction study

**Study:** Detecting distraction during online learning (undergraduate final-year project)
**Researcher:** [name, student ID, email]
**Supervisor:** [name, email]

## What you will do
You will sit at a laptop for about 15 minutes and follow on-screen instructions:
read a short lesson, use your phone, look away and talk, watch a lecture video,
stand up briefly, then study naturally. The laptop webcam records video the whole time.
No audio is recorded.

## What is kept
- The video is used only to compute numeric features (head angle, eye position,
  blink rate, hand position). **The video file is deleted once those features are
  extracted**, within 7 days of recording.
- Kept: the numeric features, the phase timestamps, and a participant code (e.g. S05).
  Your name is not stored with the data.
- Results appear in the thesis only as group statistics; no images of you are published.

## Your rights
Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time during recording, and you can ask
for your data to be removed until [date — e.g. the thesis submission/viva date]
by emailing the researcher with your participant code.
